D-Daybook Support

Help with the D-Daybook app on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Get in touch

Email ddaydeveloper.app@gmail.com with your question. Please say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

My lists or calendar are empty

D-Daybook shows your own Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders. Open the iPhone Settings app, then Apps, then D-Daybook, and check that Calendars and Reminders are allowed with full access.

My shopping list does not match my family's

Families share lists through Apple Reminders. Share the list in the Reminders app, and it will show in D-Daybook for everyone you shared it with.

My Apple Watch is out of date

Open D-Daybook on your iPhone once. Check that iCloud is switched on for D-Daybook, so the watch can catch up even when your iPhone is far away.

I have a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID and open D-Daybook. Your notes, receipts and settings come back from your own iCloud.

I want to delete my data

Delete the app. To clear the iCloud copy too, use Delete my iCloud copy in D-Daybook under Settings, iCloud. See the privacy policy for more.
